## Overview
Beginning on August of 2025, I have designed and built a nitrous-isopropanol pressure fed and pintle injected rocket motor and corrosponding test stand. This website documents the progress towards a successful hotfire, which has been achieved as of July 25th, 2026. Below are some images of the setup, and brief descriptions. Below are idealized pressure and thrust charts, as well as images of the test stand and motor. Simulations were done using Half-Cat Rocketry's public spreadsheet and NASA's CEARUN.

The overall setup has been kept as simple as possible. The motor is fead by a stacked tank arrangement - isopropanol on top, nitrous on the bottom, and a piston in between. The nitrous pressurizes the whole tank. The motor itself is a heatsink design, with a 304 steel insert in the nozzle where temperatures are highest.

![Electronics Box](assets\electronics.png)
The control board runs off of an ESP32 developer module, and is controlled wirelessly.

All valves are controlled via servos. The motor is ignited by a small solid rocket motor, and this port also serves to measure chamber pressure.
