## Test 7
Ignition was too late in test 6, leading to combustion happening downstream of nozzle. The igniter was placed further within the chamber to counteract this, but was thrown out by the propellant pressure. I would have, ideally, drilled an ignition port into the side of the chamber if the tools were available. Timing will have to be a suitable replacement.

## Test 8
Added a 3 second countdown prior to valves opening to the launch sequence, then ignited at 2 seconds remaining. Procedure seems good, but pressure was low from test 7.
I am quite confident that the next firing will be successful.
