## Test 11
Upon reviewing the footage of the first test, it appears that I got it right from the get go, that the F15 approach is a dead end. Returning to the cartridge concept yields visible mach diamonds, but the issue of poor mixing - previously chalked up to the valves not fully opening - has reared its ugly head.

## Future Improvements
This has been a long and arduous journey, and I'm not ending it with a fifth my expected performance. I suspect that the mixing issues are due to the method by which I have secured the central post of the manifold. Once the machine shop reopens 
in September, I will swap to a press-fit to ensure concentricity. Of note is the placement of the poor mixing - when reviewing the footage of Test 1, which underwent
much the same conditions, the jet of wasted fuel was in a completely different location to tests 11 and 12. From this I suspect that the issue is concentricity and not deflection under pressure.
