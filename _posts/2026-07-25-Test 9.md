## Test 9
A successful firing! The chamber pressure continues to read as zero - an oddity considering the definite thrust and exhaust behaviour, better seen in the closeup view. I will search about for a force sensor or gauge to borrow, as costs appear prohibative. The student machine shop is also currently closed, so experimentation with different nozzle geometry will have to wait. Once the shop is available I will try firing with a much smaller nozzle throat to see if I can produce Mach diamonds from overexpansion.
